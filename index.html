About Me

G'day, I'm Monty. I am an electrical engineering student based in Canberra with a deep curiosity for how complex systems work—both in technology and in society.

When I am not working through hardware problems or technical designs, I focus my energy on endurance pursuits and creative writing. Inspired by minimal travel and long-form observation, I use this site to document lessons from the road, personal philosophy, and technical projects.

Engineering

Writing

I believe in staying curious, maintaining a strong work ethic, and continually testing boundaries. Whether discussing technical innovation, sharing stories from overseas, or talking about endurance training, feel free to reach out and connect.
